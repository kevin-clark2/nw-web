Statistics indicate that 70% of the time to develop a business application is consumed by UI development. NotWired streamlines development with NotWired Components™ which save time and money on UI development.

Key Benefits

Standards Support. Built on JSP and Struts, NotWired components may be used with any development environment or application server supporting JSP 1.2 and Struts 1.1.
Faster development. Today, speed is tantamount. Taking time to develop an infrastructure does not match general business objectives. Purchasing components saves time and, in turn, save money.
Lower Maintenance Costs. Writing your own components or integrating various components from many different sources requires time and money. NotWired reduces this cost by incurring the additional maintenance burden and keeping up with changing standards.
Higher Quality Applications. NotWired Components provide your project the polish it needs to meet the sophisticated demands of your users. Component development yields higher quality applications.
Consistency. NotWired Components are designed to work as a package. By adopting a single design, your application code will be more consistent then an integration of different frameworks and components.
International Support. NotWired components externalize all labels and messages to resource bundles. It utilizes standard JSP technology to deliver international support.
Extensible. Tag libraries requiring data utilize providers to maximize flexibility. Tags may be extended per your requirements.
Ease of Integration. Just import the NotWired Components like any other tag library and use them according the JSP specification.
