NotWired's Components come in two packages - Basic and Advanced. The basic package is a good fit for general applications. The advanced package enables your project to employ calendars, activities lists, task lists, wizards, and an advanced table component.

Basic Features | Advanced Features

BreadcrumbsTag. Breadcrumb navigation utilizes a series of links to navigate through a process or web site. The component may be utilized via JSP or through a bean model defined on the server. Bean model definition allows developers to control the state and sequence of links dynamically.

Sample -> Benefits -> Features -> Advanced
WizardTag. Wizards navigate through a series of steps to reach a final submit. It is a means of gathering information over several screens. Wizards may optional use breadcrumb navigation or menu invoke actions to navigate through the steps in the wizard.

Click here for samples.
CalendarTag. Calendars offer choice of day, week, month, or multi-month views. Selections on the calendar will invoke callbacks utilizing Javascript and are compatible with any major controller framework. Samples are provided for Struts. A complete Event and CalendarView model is provided.

Click here for samples.
ActivitesTag. Activities provide a listing of events, tasks or reminders in list view. The date range of the list and filters control the view.

Click here for samples.
AnnouncementTag. Get random or current announcements from an AnnouncementProvider class and place on the screen. Developers identify an announcement provider class and the strategy of announcements.
