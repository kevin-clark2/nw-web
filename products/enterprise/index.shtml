Development of enterprise system is expensive and time consuming. NotWired lowers the cost of development and allows your organization to deliver robust Internet applications quickly.

Key Benefits

Standards Support. Built on JSP and Struts, NotWired provides the structure and pre-build high-level components necessary to build and enterprise portal.
Lower cost of development. Why invest the money building custom application frameworks. Good frameworks and business models will allow you to invest a fraction of the time and support industry standards frameworks such as JSP, Struts and Java Server Faces.
Faster development. Today, speed is king. Taking time to develop an infrastructure does not match general business objectives.
Lower Maintenance Costs. Writing your own frameworks will require time and money to maintain it and keep up with the ever changing standards. NotWired reduces this cost by accepting the additional maintenance burden.
High Quality Applications. NotWired applications and frameworks have been tested and debugged for two years. They will be higher quality than those that are built the for first time.
Consistency. NotWired is entirely layered. Applications interact with frameworks that insure a consistent implementation. This will save time and money in terms of training and the ability to move personnel from one project to another.
International Support. NotWired screens and applications externalize all lables and messages to resource bundles. It utilizes standard JSP technology to deliver international support.
Flexibility. Every layer of NotWired is designed for flexibility. Its design will allow for varied user devices and interfaces. Frameworks and applications can be adapted to custom or legacy databases.
Adaptable Datasources. NotWired delivers a service layer implementation which may utilize one or more pre-built or custom datasources to adapter to databases, message queues or any other transactional storage.
Integrated. NotWired integrates the basis for most applications on all major platforms - event scheduling, workflow, contact/customer databases and application security.
Easy to integrate. NotWired is Java libraries, JSP pages, Tablibs, and EJBs. It delivers as a WAR file which may be loaded into any standard J2EE server.
