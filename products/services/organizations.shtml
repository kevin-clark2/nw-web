Whether you are a small charitable organization, fraternity, sorority, a social club or a large national non-profit organization, NotWired provides the tools necessary to keep a centralized phone directory, plan events, share information, communicate more effectively or promote your organization.

Centralize and Share Information
Keep your staff, members and volunteers informed about events. Calendars, tasks, contacts, notes, documents and email may be shared. NotWired helps improve coordination in you organization:

  • Centralize a self-updating phonebook
  • Keep email lists always up to date
  • Keep track of contacts across your entire organization
  • Send mass-mailings to contacts
  • Manage projects by delegating and track tasks
  • Create meeting minutes and action items to make sure the proper follow ups are done
  • Notifications keep your organization members informed

Coordinate Multiple Chapters
NotWired's unique patent-pending "single dashboard" technology allows you to establish multiple organizations, yet still keep information centralized with one login. NotWired is designed to support multiple organizations, projects and teams. Shared information is only visible to team members. The NotWired multiple organization capability allows:

  • Keep calendars by organization
  • Keep contact lists by organization
  • Cross-organization searching for users that are members of multiple organizations
