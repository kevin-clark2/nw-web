Getting Started

NotWired coordinates your organization's information on-line. You can access it with a Web browser or an Internet enabled cell phone or PDA. It is fast and easy to implement but, most importantly, it will save you time and money. SIGNUP with NotWired today to receive all the benefits instantly.

Key Features ( complete list )

Personal and shared online calendars. Schedule personal and group meetings, appointments or tasks.
Invites. Many of our day-to-day activities include coordinating meetings with people who are not part of our organization. NotWired allows you to request meetings or appointments from anyone. The system will track acceptance and attendance so you always know the status of your schedule and meetings.
Task management. Tired of tracking requests from your team in e-mail? NotWired tracks task assignments to completion. Track tasks you assign to yourself or others with NotWired's task management.
Meeting minutes and action Items. Conduct effective meetings, take notes and schedule follow up on action items assigned to others or yourself.
Personal and shared contacts. Always have access to members, customers, prospects, suppliers and your personal phonebook on-line or through your Internet-enabled cell phone or PDA.
Bookmarks. Share your knowledge of web site URLs with your organization. NotWired allows bookmarks to be managed as an organization.
