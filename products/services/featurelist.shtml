Getting Started

With NotWired, it's never been easier to manage you and your team's schedule and shared information. Here is a sample of NotWired's extensive features:

  • Powerful E-mail: Full-featured email for HTML, cell phones and PDAs.
  • On-line Calendars: Manage your personal and shared group calendars on-line.
  • One-click Scheduling: Click on your calendar and schedule an event for yourself or invite others.
  • Invites: NotWired supports inviting anyone with an e-mail and allows RSVP tracking. It does not require everyone to be a member of your organization to invite them to an event.
  • Conflict Scheduling: NotWired can help detect schedule conflicts in real-time and find available time slots.
  • Repeating Events: NotWired has extensive repeat event capabilities. It even handles multiple repeats during the same week. For example, Monday, Wednesday and Friday.
  • Real-time Change Notifications: NotWired does not require you to be on the system to receive changes about meetings or events that affect your schedule. It will notify you via e-mail, pager or a message to your phone or PDA.
  • Scheduling Different Time Zones: NotWired will handle events like conference calls that may contain several people in different time zones.
  • Inbox: All new events and tasks come to your inbox which so you can accept, decline or reassign them.
  • Sharing: Allow others controlled access to your calendar. Your assistant, your spouse or anyone you choose can help manage your schedule or request time on your calendar.
  • Holidays: Create holidays for your organization so everyone knows assigned holidays.
  • Meeting Minutes and Action Items: NotWired helps you better manage your organization by providing tools to conduct more effective meetings and create action item follow ups that can be tracked.
  • Task Tracking: Track tasks on yourself and those you assigned to others.
  • Task Reassigns: NotWired allows tasks to be delegated and reassigned to others while keeping you up-to-date on the progress.
  • Contact Management: All the functionality of a standard address book plus tracking on notes, history and activities on a contact. NotWired supports private and shared contacts.
  • Group Management: Create groups that include members of your organization and/or contacts.
  • Bookmarks: Save bookmarks for personal reference or to share with the organization and other groups.
  • E-mail Bookmarks: E-mail important bookmarks to others.
  • Broadcast E-Mails: Send e-mails to contacts or groups from the web or cell phones.
  • Instant Messaging: Send a contact an instant message to their cell phone, pager or PDA.
  • Printing: NotWired provide printer friendly views of calendars and task lists
  • Wireless: NotWired has the most comprehensive wireless solution in the marketplace. Create events, manage tasks, manage contacts, access calendars and more with your cell phone of PDA.
  • General Search: Search for a key word or phrase and find the information in any part of NotWired.
  • Synchronize with PIMs: Import your contacts from Outlook, ACT! or CSV files. Export to most standard formats.
