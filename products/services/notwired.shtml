Increase management efficiency. Face it, we need more time in the day. NotWired allows you to manage your team's schedule and tasks more efficiently.
Improve communications. Tired of tracking requests from your e-mail? NotWired helps track any task assignment to completion and supports tracking tasks that may have been delegated to others.
Increase productivity. Look at your schedule anywhere. Receive real-time notifications about meetings and deadlines. Eliminate phone and e-mail tag to schedule a meeting.

Wireless access. Wireless access allows your mobile workforce to get up-to-date company information, schedule meetings and manage tasks remotely.
