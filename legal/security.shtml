Security at NotWired

Security
NotWired is committed to keeping your data safe and secure. We use state-of-the art, proven technologies and polices to protect the confidentiality of your customer data and personal information, and we will continue to update our systems to stay at the forefront of security processes and technologies.

SSL
NotWired uses the same industry-standard Secure Socket Layer (SSL) protocol that the majority of all e-commerce and financial service providers use to for purchases and financial transactions. NotWired will enable strong 128-bit SSL encryption with U.S. versions of Microsoft and Netscape browsers, and industry 40-bit SSL with non-U.S. version browsers. Each time you log in to NotWired, you can choose to use SSL to encrypt your information.

Organization ID, User ID, Passwords and PINs
We require logins to access the NotWired system. Your login information is stored encrypted in our security database behind secure firewalls which makes it unusable in the event of fraudulent activity on our site.

Firewalls and physical security
NotWired production servers are hosted at state-of-the-art data centers by multiple industry leaders. Data centers contain firewalls, monitoring and a 24-hour staff to make sure your data is safe, secure, and available to you at all times.

Access Rights
Each NotWired member is assigned access rights by their Administrator. Access rights will allow you to access information you are allowed to access but not information that is not yours. Conversely, no one can access your data without access rights. Only your organization ID, user ID and password combination in our three-key authentication system will allow you access to your data.
