What kind of security does NotWired have to protect your information?

We believe in strong security. Here are some steps we have taken to protect and preserve your information:

  • All access to NotWired systems is user ID and password protected so only you or your administrator have access to your personal information.
  • All our cookies that we place on your browser have strong encryption to protect your information in case your computer is lost or files are stolen.
  • Options exist to logout completely which will remove all cookies and make it impossible for someone to sit down on the same computer and use your account. We recommend complete logout or not to save user id and password information when on public computers.
  • Our production servers are locked in a room with access by limited personnel.
  • All information is stored on servers behind a firewall.
  • Every night your information is remotely backed up to a different location than the NotWired offices.
  • We periodically hire security experts to break into our systems.

Internet data transmission is not 100% secure. We strive to protect your personal information on our systems. No matter how much we strive to be secure, NotWired can not ensure or warrant the security of any information you transmit to us or from our online products or services, and you do so at your own risk.

What are cookies and how does NotWired use them?

NotWired and various portions of the services use cookies to  (1) save your password so you do not have to re-enter it each time you log in; (2) deliver content specific to your interests; and (3) keep track of your account state while you are using our services or visiting NotWired. Your information in cookies is encrypted to prevent unwanted sites from gathering your personal information. If you set your browser to reject cookies, you will not be able to access or use the services.
