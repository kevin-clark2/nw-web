What is NotWired?

Improved communication and collaboration, web and wireless based accessibility.

Who is it designed for?
NotWired is a system designed for small and medium sized groups and businesses who want to improve communications and collaboration without investing in expensive groupware software.

How does it work?
Using a web browser or wireless access, employees, suppliers, customers, friends and family can better coordinate activities using Internet. Users can choose how and who they wish to share information with through the NotWired system.

Who uses it?
Every member of your organization or external contact that needs to communicate, collaborate and share information and events with your organization can use the NotWired system. Sophisticated access control lets you decide on what and what not to share.

Benefits
NotWired creates a single source of event and organization information to improve communication and collaboration. The results: time savings, cost savings, more effective meetings, optimized team performance, less IT staff dependencies, access from anywhere and uninterrupted growth.
