What is Web-based email? Is it secure?
Web-based email is electronic mail that can be sent and received from a Web site on the Internet, using a Web browser (like Netscape Navigator or Internet Explorer), rather than using an email program (such as Qualcomm Communications' Eudora� or Microsoft Outlook�). The advantage of Web-based email is that it can be retrieved and sent from any computer with an Internet connection, whereas traditional email programs must be configured on a specific computer to send and receive mail for a particular email address.

NotWired makes security a priority. NotWired's Email Service utilizes state-of-the-art security measures in its mail system architecture. What do "Cc:" and "Bcc:" mean ?

Cc: stands for "carbon copy." Anyone listed in the Cc: field of a message will receive a copy of that message when you send it. All other recipients of that message will be able to see that the person you designated as a Cc: recipient has received a copy of the message.

Bcc: stands for "blind carbon copy." This is similar to the Cc: feature, except that Bcc: recipients are invisible to all of the other recipients of the message (including other Bcc: recipients). For example, if you send a message To: johnsmith@notwired.com and Bcc: janesmith@notwired.com, then johnsmith will see himself as the message's only recipient, while janesmith will see that you have also sent the message to johnsmith. To send a message, you must always specify at least one recipient in the To: field. If you don't, an error message will appear when you attempt to send the message.

Please note: Sending unsolicited mail or "spam" is a violation of the NotWired Mail Terms of Service and won't be tolerated.

How do I reply to a message?
To reply to a message that you have received in your NotWired Mail account, you must first open the message on your screen. When the message is open:

1. Click the "Reply" link at the top of the message window to reply only to the sender of the message. If you would like to send your reply to all of the other recipients of the message in addition to the sender, click the "Reply All" link.

2. A Compose Mail window will appear. If you clicked the "Reply" link, the sender's email address will appear in the "To:" field. If you clicked the "Reply All" link, then in addition to the sender's address, the addresses of all of the other recipients will appear in the "To:" and/ or "Cc:" fields, according to the way they were listed in the original message. You can also add new email addresses if you wish.

3. Depending on your "Preferences" setting*, in the large text window you will see quoted all, part, or none of the sender's message. A quoted message will be set off by ">" symbols on the left margin.

4. Type your reply. If you have quoted the sender's message, you can type in the area above the sender's message as well within the sender's message itself.

5. Click the "Send" button to send your reply.

Can I save an unfinished e-mail as a draft without sending it?
To save an unfinished email composition without sending it, click the "Save to Drafts" button near the top of the Compose Mail window.

Once you have saved a draft, you can open it at any time by doing the following:
  1. Choose the "Folders" selection from the View combo box on the top left portion of the mail application.
  2. Choose the "Drafts" folder from the folder list and click on it.
  3. Click on the message you wish to continue composing.

Saving draft messages is an excellent way to create templates for form letters. Messages saved in your Draft folder will remain there until you delete them.

Also, if you attach a file to a composition and then save it as a draft, the text of your composition will be saved, but the attached file will not. You will need to reattach the file before sending the message.

If I send a message to someone, how can I tell if they've received and read it?
Currently, it is not possible to tell if others have received the messages that you've sent to them.

However, if you don't receive your email back with an error message, you can be certain that it has been received by your recipient's mail system. Unfortunately, this doesn't indicate that the message has been opened or read.

How can I add an attachment to a message?
You can send all types of files as attachments, including word processor or spreadsheet documents, audio files, image files (.bmp, .jpg., .gif, etc.), web pages saved as HTML files, and more. Attachments are limited to 2MB in size unless you pay for premium NotWired services.

To add an attachment to a message, follow the instructions below for your browser and version.

Netscape Users
First, log into NotWired and begin composing a message. Once you've done this:

1. Click the "Browse" button and select a file.
2. When you have selected a file, click the "Add Attachment" button to attach it to your message.
3. To add multiple attachments, repeat
steps 1 and 2.
4. To delete an attachment, click the Detach link.

Once the files are attached, you can address, compose, and send the message as you normally would.

Internet Explorer Users
You must use version 3.02 or later of Internet Explorer in order to send attachments. If you use Internet Explorer 4.x, 5.x, 6.x or if you have installed the upload patch for version 3.02, add an attachment by doing the following:

1. Log into your NotWired Mail account and begin composing a message.
2. Click the "Browse" button and select a file.
3. When you have selected a file, click the "Add Attachment" button to attach it to your message.
4. To add multiple attachments, repeat
steps 2 and 3.
6. To delete an attachment, click the Detach link.

Once the files are attached, you can address, compose, and send the message as you normally would.

Other Browsers
Depending on your browser and version, you may be able to follow the instructions for Netscape users above. If not, please consult your browser's help file for information regarding file uploads.

Why do I get "quota exceeded" message when I use email?
NotWired imposes limits on the size and number of messages you can store on our email servers. In order to keep your storage under quota do the following:

NotWired Web Client:
Select messages and use "Delete Selected" link to permanently delete messages. You will not be able to move messages into "Trash" folder since it is included when measuring quota.

Other IMAP Clients(Outlook, Outlook Express, etc...):
Select messages and use "Delete" option on the "Edit" menu. Use "Purge Deleted Messages" option on the "Edit" menu to permanently delete messages. You will not be able to move message into "Trash" folder since it is included when measuring quota.
