Using NotWired from your phone
From most mobile phones, it is possible to get to any WAP enabled URL. To access NotWired, you must enter the WAP URL which is www.notwired.com. This will require you to type the URL address on your phone using the numeric keypad. Please refer to the directions on your phone to set a URL using your phone browser. For U.S. phones, arrow down until you get to the "go to..." link. This may require you to link through a couple of "more..." links. See directions on your phone for selection of a link.

You type letters on the phone by pressing the numeric equivelents one to three times. By typing on the phone, you can enter www.notwired.com as the URL to access NotWired. Setting bookmarks on your phone
After you have entered the NotWired URL as described above, you may bookmark the URL for future reference. The NotWired logo and site will appear on your phone. Press and hold the "Menu" or "Function" key of your phone until a menu appears. This is the Browser Menu and will allow you to "Mark Site" which will save the bookmark on your phone. The "Mark Site" feature will allow you to customize the name of the bookmark. By default, NotWired is "NotWired".

Viewing bookmarks on your phone
Most carriers have a home menu that will appear whenever you start you phone's Internet browser. Additionally, the all have some bookmark option within their menus. Arrow down to the bookmarks and select it. A list of bookmarks will appear. You can arrow down to "NotWired" and select it. This will bring you to the NotWired login.
