Using NotWired from your Palm Pilot

  1. Download the NotWired.pqa file onto your desktop.
  2. Double click on your "Palm Desktop" icon on your desktop.
  3. Click on the "Install" icon.
  4. In the user drop-down list, select the name that corresponds to your organizer.
  5. Click on the "add" icon and locate the NotWired.pqa file you just downloaded.
  6. Click on the "Open" button. Click on the "Done" button.
  7. Insert your Palm Pilot into the cradle and turn it on.
  8. Press the HOTSYNC button on the cradle Wait for a message on your organizer indicating that the process is complete.
  9. The NotWired icon should appear on your Palm Pilot screen.

    10. NOTE: If you are upgrading or reinstalling the NotWired pqa file, you delete the NotWired application before reinstalling.
