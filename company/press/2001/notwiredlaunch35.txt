


NOTWIRED RELEASES VERSION 3.5 WITH INTEGRATED EMAIL INTO ITS MOBILE OFFICE

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

CHICAGO, ILLINOIS � November 5, 2001 -- NotWired, Inc. (www.notwired.com) today announces the launch of version 3.5 of the NotWired Mobile Office, adding advanced integrated email to its powerful  contact management and scheduling solution. The entire suite of tools immediately enables an instant mobile office with no setup costs.  NotWired Mobile Office offers business-class email, group scheduling, SFA and workflow management for mobile professionals, accessible via the Internet and wireless devices. 

What puts this service above other email solutions?  NotWired integrates email directly with personal and shared contact management to enable individuals and businesses to track communications on contacts. Email also works seamlessly with group scheduling and task management, for optimal productivity. 

NotWired users will now be able to keep a database of all their contacts, add notes and activities to these contacts and track email correspondence. Users can rapidly access email and contacts with one-touch dialing or short messaging on their wireless PDAs and phones.
