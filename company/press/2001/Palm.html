NOTWIRED ANNOUNCES AVAILABILITY ON PALM™ HANDHELD COMPUTERS WITH WIRELESS SERVICE

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

CHICAGO, IL - January 05, 2000 - NotWired, Inc. (http://www.notwired.com) today announces the availability of NotWired Mobile Business Service on Palm™ handheld computers with PQA. NotWired supplies completely integrated wireless and wireline group scheduling and process management for mobile professionals. The service targets small and emerging enterprises, and professional organizations with groupware and collaboration tools to manage scheduling and sharing of information.

"NotWired today announces the only peer-to-peer scheduling system. You will no longer need a computer to create meetings and appointments with others," said Kevin Clark, President and CEO of NotWired. "The addition of NotWired service on Palm™ handheld computers strengthens our offerings to mobile professionals."

Expanded mobility with PQA service
With today’s mobile workforce, scheduling and management of corporate information is a knowledge workers main tool. NotWired on PQA empowers mobile professionals to create meetings, invites, or events; view personal task lists; and view daily calendars. An integrated inbox consolidates the user’s tasks and meeting requests expediting workflow while user is on the road.

Some powerful features of NotWired on PQA are:

  • Peer to peer and peer to group event creation, invites and sharing.
  • Wireless preference control - select when and where (PC or wireless device) notifications will be sent.
  • Support for Palm™ -handheld computers with wireless service.

About NotWired
 NotWired is a leading application service provider creating applications to empower the mobile professional. The services are specifically designed for the convergence of the Internet with wireless access.
