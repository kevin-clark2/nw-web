NOTWIRED RELEASES VERSION 3.1 WITH INTEGRATED SFA

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

CHICAGO, IL - June 1, 2001 - NotWired, Inc. (www.notwired.com) today announces the launch of version 3.5 of the NotWired Mobile Office, adding advanced integrated email to its powerful contact management and scheduling solution. The entire suite of tools immediately enables an instant mobile office with no setup costs. NotWired Mobile Office offers business-class email, group scheduling, SFA and workflow management for mobile professionals, accessible via the Internet and wireless devices.

What puts this service above other email solutions? NotWired integrates email directly with personal and shared contact management to enable individuals and businesses to track communications on contacts. Email also works seamlessly with group scheduling and task management, for optimal productivity.

NotWired users will now be able to keep a database of all their contacts, add notes and activities to these contacts and track email correspondence. Users can rapidly access email and contacts with one-touch dialing or short messaging on their wireless PDAs and phones.

"By integrating advanced contact management, scheduling and email, NotWired now provides a single integrated solution to manage all communication, scheduling, tasks, and contact management for both the individual and organizations in a groupware system," said Kevin Clark, President and CEO of NotWired, Inc.

Manage Email While Mobile
NotWired supports devices with WAP and Palm™ handheld computers with wireless services. Professionals can use their time more efficiently with NotWired's wireless solution. While waiting at airport or for a meeting, users check email and manage their personal information. NotWired supports full compose, reply, replyall and forward capabilities for email. It also supports folders so you can organize your email before you return to the office.

Optimal Solution for Managing Small and Emerging Enterprises
Most small and emerging businesses cannot afford costly enterprise software solutions, or the IT staff needed to install and maintain such solutions. NotWired provides all hosting and maintenance for a minimal monthly fee, so professionals can focus more efforts on business and not internal computer systems.
