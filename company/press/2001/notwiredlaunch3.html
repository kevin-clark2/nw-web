NOTWIRED RELEASES VERSION 3.0 - ADVANCED CRM AND CALENDAR SHARING

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

CHICAGO, IL - March 29, 2001 - NotWired, Inc. (www.notwired.com) today announces the launch of version 3.0 of the NotWired Mobile Office, adding proxy capabilities to their calendar and scheduling service and advanced CRM capabilities.

NotWired users can grant proxies permission to view and add events to their personal calendars. Managers can establish their assistant as a proxy, enabling the assistant to view the manager's schedule and add events, expediting the scheduling process.

"Adding sharing to our calendar and scheduling service broadens the scope of scheduling capabilities," said Kevin Clark, President and CEO of NotWired, Inc. "Now assistants or colleagues granted permissions can rapidly schedule meetings and events to manage other members calendars. Shared calendar management saves a few steps in the scheduling process, which is critical for busy executives."

A Complete Scheduling Solution
NotWired offers the most comprehensive mobile office service on the market. Key features of this service include personal and shared online calendars, meeting planning, scheduling conflict detection, meeting management, group task management and shared contact management with CRM.

Optimal Solution for Managing Small and Emerging Enterprises
Organizations, especially small and emerging enterprises, need software and services like NotWired's Mobile Office to help effectively and efficiently manage their business. Most small enterprises cannot afford costly enterprise software solutions, or the IT staff needed to install and maintain such solutions. For as low as $9.95/month, NotWired offers a cost-effective alternative. And NotWired provides all hosting and maintenance, so professionals can focus more efforts on business and not internal computer systems.
