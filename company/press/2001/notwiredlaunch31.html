NOTWIRED RELEASES VERSION 3.1 WITH INTEGRATED SFA INTO ITS MOBILE OFFICE

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

CHICAGO, IL - June 1, 2001 - NotWired, Inc. (www.notwired.com) today announces the launch of version 3.1 of the NotWired Mobile Office, adding contact management and CRM tools to the suite of wired and wireless business solutions. NotWired Mobile Office offers fully-integrated group scheduling and workflow management for mobile professionals, accessible via the Internet and wireless devices.

NotWired users will now be able to keep a database of all their contacts, add notes and activities to these contacts, and have rapid access to contacts with one-touch dialing or short messaging on their wireless PDAs and phones. Users can import and export data from Palm, ACT!, and Outlook.

What puts this service above other CRM solutions? NotWired integrates the contact management tools with other NotWired services, such as group scheduling and task management, for optimal productivity. Members can schedule meetings with contacts, attaching meeting agendas and following up with meeting minutes and action items. "By integrating contact management and CRM, NotWired now provides a single integrated solution to manage all scheduling, tasks, and contacts for both the individual and organizations in a groupware system," said Kevin Clark, President and CEO of NotWired, Inc.

A Complete Scheduling Solution
NotWired supports devices with WAP, Palm™ handheld computers with wireless service and WAP browsers so busy professionals can manage contacts while mobile. Professionals can use their time more efficiently with NotWired’s wireless solution. While waiting at airport, members can schedule calls with contacts for the next day. While returning from a customer, NotWired users can insert notes in the contact’s history, which can be instantly viewed by other members back at the office or in other locations.

Optimal Solution for Managing Small and Emerging Enterprises
Most small and emerging businesses cannot afford costly enterprise software solutions, or the IT staff needed to install and maintain such solutions. NotWired provides all hosting and maintenance for a minimal monthly fee, so professionals can focus more efforts on business and not internal computer systems.

About NotWired, Inc.
NotWired is a leading application service provider creating applications to empower the mobile professional. The services are specifically designed for the convergence of the Internet with wireless access.
