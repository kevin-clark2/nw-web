NOTWIRED RELEASES VERSION 4.0 WITH
NATIVE PALM™ APPLICATIONS

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

CHICAGO, IL - NotWired, Inc. (www.notwired.com) today announces the launch of version 4.1 of the NotWired Mobile Office, adding native Palm support to its mobile groupware solution. The entire suite of tools immediately enables an instant mobile office with no setup costs. NotWired Mobile Office offers business-class email, group scheduling, SFA and workflow management for mobile professionals all accessible via the Internet, wireless devices and PDAs.

What puts this service above other solutions? NotWired integrates email directly with personal and shared contact management to enable individuals and businesses to track communications on contacts. Email also works seamlessly with group scheduling and task management, for optimal productivity.

Now, Palm users will now be able to create event invitations and assign tasks directly from their native Palm DateBook and ToDo List applications. With virtually no learning curve for existing Palm users, NotWired has enabled robust groupware using NotWired’s service with Palm PDAs.

“By enhancing basic Palm applications, NotWired enables Palm users to create events with invitations and assign tasks to others directly on their native Palm applications. The applications can be used disconnected from the Internet, with virtually no learning curve for existing Palm users. NotWired’s Palm Coordinator enables power Palm users to enjoy group scheduling and task management with their familiar Palm applications,” said Kevin Clark, President and CEO of NotWired, Inc.

Management In The Palm Of Your Hand
NotWired supports devices with WAP and Palm™ handheld computers. Professionals can use their time more efficiently with NotWired’s groupware solution. While waiting at an airport or for a meeting, users can check email and manage their personal information. The new applications from NotWired support scheduling invitations and task assignment and tracking. Busy people enhance their productivity immediately with NotWired.

Optimal Solution for Small and Emerging Businesses
Most small and emerging businesses cannot afford costly enterprise software solutions, or the IT staff needed to install and maintain such solutions. NotWired provides all hosting and maintenance for a minimal monthly fee, so professionals can focus more efforts on business and not internal computer systems.

About NotWired
NotWired is a leading application service provider that empowers the mobile professional. NotWired’s services are specifically designed for the convergence of the Internet with wireless access on phones and PDAs.
