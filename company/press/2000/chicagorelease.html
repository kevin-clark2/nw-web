CHICAGO-BASED COMPANY ANNOUNCES NOTWIRED, A COMPLETELY INTEGRATED WIRELESS APPLICATION PORTAL ESPECIALLY DESIGNED FOR SMALL BUSINESS

Applications That Empower Your Mobile Lifestyle

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

CHICAGO, IL -  May 8, 2000 - Objectsoft, Inc. (www.objsoft.com) announced today the launch of NotWired (www.notwired.com). NotWired is an entirely integrated application for small to medium-sized companies with complete wireless access.

The NotWired service enables organizations and their mobile professionals to seamlessly utilize productivity and business applications via PCs and wireless devices. Group scheduling, reminders, meetings, messages and more, are now available at (www.notwired.com). Additional modules will be added in the coming months to enhance the productivity of sales, administration and travel management. NotWired applications work interactively with WAP-enabled phones and other wireless devices.

"The key focus of NotWired is to empower the growing company’s mobile business professional with real-time access to tightly integrated productivity, content and business applications over the Internet. The NotWired integrated applications are targeted for the small and medium business marketplace because integrated application suites are too expensive and time consuming to install and maintain," said Kevin Clark, CEO of Objectsoft. " NotWired was created, from the beginning, with the objective to empower the rest of us."

Optimal Solution for Managing Small Business

Organizations, and especially small and medium-sized companies, need software and services such as NotWired to help effectively and efficiently operate their business. Sales, human resources, communications, marketing, contact management, lead generation, customer support, real-time information gathering are all in a days work for a small and growing business. Unlike large corporations, smaller, growing companies have to rely on various vendors with specialized offerings. These different offerings generally do not support integration with other applications nor offer integrated wireless solutions. The cost for small businesses to create suitable systems is prohibitive.

NotWired’s application and data integration is provided through a centralized group calendar system and employee database designed to address the complex scenarios of coordinating all contacts with scheduling. Since it is built on a solid foundation of security that prevents unauthorized users from viewing sensitive data or performing sensitive operations. Wireless and Internet access makes NotWired accessible from anywhere, at anytime, and is designed for one-touch ease of use.

NotWired Offers Services Companies Require

NotWired enhances the collaboration of communications worldwide. Here are a few examples of the NotWired services that businesses demand:

Workflow inbox: Get everything in an email or mobile inbox. From this inbox, delegate to others or respond directly with a phone.

Groupware calendar: Create personal, organization or events calendars. All calendars are integrated with security, so information can be public or private. Daily and weekly views are available.

Groupware to-dos: NotWired allows the creation of to-dos, assignment to others and ability to track the progress to completion.

Groupware contacts: Personal contact list, company contact list, or any other contact list that needs to be created.

Global find: Find all information in your NotWired system or information on the Internet.
