NOTWIRED DEBUTS AT PCIA CHICAGO

Applications That Empower Your Mobile Lifestyle

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

CHICAGO, IL - September 8, 2000 - After a year of development, NotWired will be exhibiting in booth #4417 during the PCIA Global Exchange in Chicago from September 26-29. For its first public appearance, NotWired (www.notwired.com) will showcase its entirely integrated wireless and wired scheduling and collaboration solution. The NotWired solution is targeted for small to emerging companies whose mobile professionals need access to personal and corporate information anywhere at anytime.

The NotWired service enables organizations and their mobile professionals to seamlessly utilize productivity and business applications via PCs and wireless devices. Group scheduling, reminders, meetings, messages and other business productivity applications, are now available at www.notwired.com.  NotWired applications work interactively with Internet browsers, WAP-enabled phones and other wireless devices.

"NotWired will be launching at the PCIA show in Chicago the most powerful wireless and wired scheduling and collaboration in the marketplace," said Kevin Clark, CEO of NotWired. " NotWired was created, from the beginning, to empower the lifestyles of mobile professionals. We will debut NotWired and some amazing features in the next release due out fall 2000."

Optimal Solution for Managing Small and Emerging Business

Organizations, and especially small and emerging companies, need software and services such as NotWired to help effectively and efficiently operate their business. Sales, human resources, communications, marketing, contact management, lead generation, customer support and real-time information gathering is all in a days work for a small and growing business. Unlike large corporations, smaller, growing companies have to rely on various vendors with specialized offerings. These different offerings generally do not support integration with other applications nor offer integrated wireless solutions. The cost for small businesses to create suitable systems is prohibitive.

NotWired’s application and data integration is provided through a centralized group calendar system and employee database designed to address the complex scenarios of coordinating all contacts with scheduling. Since NotWired is built on a solid foundation of security, it prevents unauthorized users from viewing sensitive data or performing sensitive operations. Wireless and Internet access makes NotWired accessible from anywhere to empower the mobile professional.
