WIRELESS COMPANY NOTWIRED LAUNCHES

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

CHICAGO, IL - September 5, 2000 - Objectsoft (www.objsoft.com) today announces the launch of NotWired as a separate company. NotWired is the creator of the wireless portal for mobile professionals (www.notwired.com). For the last year, NotWired operated within Objectsoft to address the needs of mobile professionals. It is an entirely integrated wireless and wired scheduling and collaboration solution for small and emerging companies.

"Today's launch establishes NotWired as a Delaware corporation to facilitate professional investment." said Kevin Clark, CEO of NotWired and Objectsoft. "By establishing NotWired as its own entity, it will allow both companies to grow and focus on their respective markets with the proper structure to facilitate growth."

NotWired, Optimal Solutions for Managing Business

Organizations, and especially small and emerging companies, need software and services such as NotWired to help effectively and efficiently operate their business. Sales, human resources, communications, marketing, contact management, lead generation, customer support, real-time information gathering is all in a days work for a small and growing business. Unlike large corporations, smaller, growing companies have to rely on various vendors with specialized offerings. These different offerings generally do not support integration with other applications nor offer integrated wireless solutions. The cost for small businesses to create suitable systems is prohibitive.

NotWired’s application and data integration is provided through a centralized group calendar system and employee database designed to address the complex scenarios of coordinating all contacts with scheduling. Since it is built on a solid foundation of security that prevents unauthorized users from viewing sensitive data or performing sensitive operations. Wireless and Internet access makes NotWired accessible from anywhere, at anytime, and is designed for one-touch ease of use.
