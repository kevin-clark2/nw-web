NOTWIRED OFFERS GROUP SCHEDULING FOR THE MAC AND UNIX

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

CHICAGO, IL - December 5, 2000 - NotWired, Inc. (http://www.notwired.com/) today announces the supported availability of the NotWired Mobile Business Service on the Mac and UNIX platforms. NotWired supplies completely integrated real-time group scheduling and process management for mobile professionals, accessible via the Web and wireless devices. The service targets small and emerging enterprises, and professional organizations with groupware and collaboration tools to manage scheduling and sharing of information.

"NotWired is pleased to announce general availability on all platforms," said Kevin Clark, President and CEO of NotWired. "Every system has unique features and characteristics that must be incorporated into design to yield the best end-user experience. As Mac is one of the major desktop platforms, we have spent the time and effort to optimize the experience for Mac users, who rely on first-rate services. With the addition of availability on the Mac platform, NotWired can confidently say we are successfully deploying on all desktop platforms and operating systems along with all major wireless devices."

"The Macintosh platform provides professional customers with truly great and innovative mobile products," said Clent Richardson, Apple's vice president of Worldwide Developer Relations. "Apple is pleased that NotWired, with its NotWired Mobile Business Service, is providing Mac mobile and small business professionals a new tool to help do their jobs successfully."

As the only enterprise-class web and wireless group scheduling system, NotWired enables small and emerging enterprises with an alternative to purchasing, installing and operating their own systems. It is the lowest cost and most robust solution for an organization to acquire enterprise-class scheduling and collaboration complete with web and wireless access.

Optimal Solutions for Managing Small and Emerging Enterprises
Organizations, and especially small and emerging enterprises, need software and services like NotWired to help effectively and efficiently manage their business. With today’s mobile workforce, scheduling and management of corporate information is a knowledge worker’s main tool. Unlike large corporations, smaller, growing enterprises do not have the resources to create integrated wireless solutions. NotWired is an affordable service to empower mobile professionals. About NotWired NotWired is a leading application service provider creating applications to empower the mobile professional. The services are specifically designed for the convergence of the Internet with wireless access.
