NOTWIRED ANNOUNCES WIRELESS APPLICATION SERVICES FOR CARRIERS AND TOP WEB SITES

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

CHICAGO, IL – September 25, 2000 – NotWired (www.notwired.com) announced today that they have signed an agreement with UUNet, a leader in complex Internet server hosting and management. NotWired, a wireless Internet Application Service Provider will deliver a series of solutions for the mobile professional. By working with UUNET, NotWiredt can be confident that is wireless customers are being furnished with the essential connectivity they need.

The NotWired service will enable mobile professionals to seamlessly utilize productivity and business applications throught the PCs and wireless devices. The service is a combination of standard portal and wireless portal applications to support simple and complex business processing. Group scheduling, reminders, meetings, messages and other applications are now available at www.notwired.com . NotWired is unique since it is designed to be groupware and not just consumer based applications and content.

By locating NotWired services within UUNET, a state-of-the-art Internet Data Centers (IDCs), NotWiredt strengthens its commitment to providing the most secure, reliable, scalable and accessible applications and services for the increasingly diverse wireless Internet industry. The combination of UUNET's  geographic distribution, scalable bandwidth, and 24x7 application monitoring is intended to ensure that NotWiredt's services and content are readily available to its growing customer base.
