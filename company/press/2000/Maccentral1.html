NotWired offers group scheduling for the Mac
Staff Report, email@maccentral.com
December 5, 2000, 3:40 pm ET

NotWired Inc., an application service provider that creates applications to empower the mobile professional, has announced the availability of the NotWired Mobile Business Service on the Mac and UNIX platforms.

NotWired supplies integrated real-time group scheduling and process management for mobile professionals, accessible via the Web and wireless devices. The service targets small and emerging enterprises, and professional organizations with groupware and collaboration tools to manage scheduling and sharing of information.

"NotWired is pleased to announce general availability on all platforms,'' says Kevin Clark, president and CEO of NotWired, in a press announcement. "Every system has unique features and characteristics that must be incorporated into design to yield the best end-user experience.

"As Mac is one of the major desktop platforms, we have spent the time and effort to optimize the experience for Mac users, who rely on first-rate services. With the addition of availability on the Mac platform, NotWired can confidently say we are successfully deploying on all desktop platforms and operating systems along with all major wireless devices.''

As the only enterprise-class Web and wireless group scheduling system, NotWired enables small and emerging enterprises with an alternative to purchasing, installing, and operating their own systems, he says. It is the lowest cost and most robust solution for an organization to acquire enterprise-class scheduling and collaboration complete with web and wireless access, adds Clark.
