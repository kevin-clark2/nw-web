NOTWIRED ANNOUNCES THE LAUNCH OF NOTWIRED 2.0 – GROUP SCHEDULING FOR SMALL AND EMERGING ENTERPRISES

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

CHICAGO, IL - November 29, 2000 - NotWired, Inc. (http://www.notwired.com/) today announces the launch of version 2.0 of the NotWired Mobile Business service. NotWired offers fully-integrated, real-time group scheduling, task and program management for mobile professionals, accessible via the Web and wireless devices. This service targets small and emerging enterprises, and professional organizations with groupware and collaboration tools to manage scheduling and sharing of information.

NotWired mobilizes small and emerging enterprises with services that allow groups and teams to communicate and coordinate more efficiently anytime, anywhere. Key features of this service include an online group calendar, the ability to schedule external invitees, meeting minutes and action items for improved task management. A shared organization phonebook and shared bookmarks improve overall management of organizations.

"NotWired raises the bar on group scheduling. As an enterprise-class web and wireless group scheduling system, NotWired enables small and emerging enterprises with an alternative to purchasing, installing and operating their own systems." said Kevin Clark, President and CEO of NotWired. "NotWired is the lowest cost solution for an organization to acquire enterprise-class scheduling and collaboration complete with web and wireless access."

Optimal Solution for Managing Small and Emerging Enterprises

Organizations, and especially small and emerging companies, need software and services such as NotWired to help effectively and efficiently manage their business. Unlike large corporations, smaller, growing companies have to rely on various vendors with specialized offerings. These different offerings generally do not support integration with other applications nor offer integrated wireless solutions. The cost to create a system to service the mobile workforce is prohibitive. NotWired is an integrated service with complete wireless access to empower small and emerging enterprises.

Some powerful features added to NotWired in the release of version 2.0 are:

  • Wireless task management. Now busy managers can manage while mobile.
  • Wireless meeting management including minutes and action items.
  • Wireless task reassigns.
  • Enhanced control over notifications.
  • Enhanced wireless interface support for easy one-touch access to communicate.
  • Instant messaging to event invitees, workgroups or anyone in the contact list.
  • Conflict detection and resolution for invitees to determine the best possible times to meet.
  • iCal and vCard to integrate Lotus Notes, ACT!, Outlook™ and other scheduling systems.
  • Support for RIM™ devices with WAP.
  • Support for Palm™ handheld computers with wireless service and a WAP browser.
