OBJECTSOFT LAUNCHES NOTWIRED.COM, A COMPLETELY INTEGRATED WIRELESS APPLICATION PORTAL

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

CHICAGO, IL – February 25, 2000 – Objectsoft, Inc. (www.objsoft.com) announced today the beta launch of NotWired.com (www.notwired.com) which is a complete wireless portal for business professionals.

The NotWired.com service will enable mobile professionals to seamlessly utilize productivity and business applications via PCs and wireless devices. The service is a combination of standard portal and wireless portal applications to support simple and complex business processing. Group scheduling, reminders, meetings, messages and more, are now available at www.notwired.com. Additional modules will be added in the coming months to enhance the productivity of accounting, administration and travel. NotWired.com is unique because it is designed to be groupware for small and medium businesses and not just single user consumer applications.

NotWired.com applications work interactively with WAP enabled phones and integrate through SMS to the existing 250 million digital PCS phones, pagers and devices in the marketplace today. WAP enabled phones enjoy interactive applications that directly access the centralized portal database so all information in the organization is always up-to-date.

"The key focus of NotWired.com is to empower the mobile business professional with real-time access to tightly integrated productivity, content and business applications over the Internet. The NotWired.com integrated applications are targeted for the small and medium business marketplace where integrated application suites are expensive and time consuming to install and maintain," said Kevin Clark, CEO of Objectsoft. "NotWired applications deliver the future of computing to users today."

About Objectsoft

Objectsoft is a leading developer of mobile application frameworks that enable development of secure wireless and wired applications packaged for billable delivery via the Internet. Objectsoft is focused on business applications that empower the mobile professional.
