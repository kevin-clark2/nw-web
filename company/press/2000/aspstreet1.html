NotWired Launches ASP Service for Palms
January 8, 2001
This article is not rated yet.

Send to friend  Printer-friendly  Subscribe to ASPstreet.com newsletter
 
 

NotWired, Inc. has launched its NotWired Mobile Business Service for Palm handheld computers, providing wireless and wireline group scheduling and process management for mobile professionals.

The service targets small and emerging enterprises, as well as professional organizations with groupware and collaboration tools to manage scheduling and sharing of information.

NotWired called it the "only peer-to-peer scheduling system," eliminating the need for a computer to create meetings and appointments with others. The service allows users to create meetings, invitations or events; view personal task lists; and view daily calendars.
