NOTWIRED 2.5 - FIRST PEER TO PEER WIRELESS SCHEDULING

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

CHICAGO, IL - December 7, 2000 - NotWired, Inc. (www.notwired.com) today announces the launch of version 2.5 of the NotWired service. NotWired supplies completely integrated wireless and wired group scheduling and process management for mobile professionals. The service targets small and emerging enterprises, and professional organizations with groupware and collaboration tools to manage scheduling and sharing of information.

"NotWired today announces the only peer to peer scheduling system. You will no longer need a computer to create meetings and appointments with others," said Kevin Clark, President and CEO of NotWired. "As the only enterprise-class web and wireless group scheduling system, NotWired enables small and emerging enterprises with an alternative to purchasing, installing and operating their own systems. It is the lowest cost and most robust solution for an organization to acquire enterprise-class scheduling and collaboration complete with web and wireless access."

Optimal Solution for Managing Small and Emerging Enterprises

Organizations, and especially small and emerging enterprises, need software and services such as NotWired to help effectively and efficiently manage their business. With today's mobile workforce, scheduling and management of corporate information is a knowledge workers main tool. Unlike large corporations, smaller, growing enterprises do not have the resources to create integrated wireless solutions. NotWired is an affordable service to empower mobile professionals.

Some powerful features added to NotWired in this release are:

  • Peer to peer and peer to group event creation, invites and sharing.
  • Smart event creation that will remember who you interact with the most.
  • Wireless preference control.
  • Support for Palm™ devices with PQA.

About NotWired

NotWired is a leading application service provider creating applications to empower the mobile professional. The services are specifically designed for the convergence of the Internet with wireless access.
