Marketing
Marketing involves research, coordination and execution. NotWired assists a marketing team by providing a central, shared repository of bookmarks that can be used to track competition, tradeshows, media and more... The result, better collaboration and execution.

Consulting and Sales
Consultants and sales representatives are "road" warriors. NotWired's company phonebook and scheduling system are available on any computer or any Internet cell phone or PDA.  NotWired allows mobile employees to always be in touch with the office and their colleagues. Forget email tag to schedule a meeting, NotWired has full group scheduling to coordinate  over the Internet. Forget paper phone lists that are always out of date. On NotWired, a company phonebook is accessible from the Internet or any wireless Internet device. The result, instant access to information when needed.

Real Estate
Real estate agents can schedule clients through NotWired's unique scheduling system. It allows anyone on the Internet or a WAP enabled cell phone to participate in scheduling activities with NotWired members. Once scheduled, the NotWired system will send a reminder to the client about the appointment. Location can be specified with appointments so clients can 'one-click' retreive a map from the invitation or reminder. The result, better communication and scheduling.

Executive Management
Executives are busy and on-the-go.  Management involves communication and delegation of tasks. Tasks can be used to manage projects or people. Forget emails to track all the requests you made! Executives can assign tasks and check the status of tasks from a PC or wireless device. Employees share a common list of tasks by project and management always has up-to-date information about the status of projects or tasks. The result, better management and operations.
