What is groupware?

Groupware, loosely defined, is any software that allows groups to communicate better. Email, scheduling, task management, chats, newgroups, project collaboration, shared phonebooks, and shared contacts are all groupware.

Groupware becomes an integrated information system when it solves real business problems effectively and efficiently around a business process. To accomplish this capability, the Global 2000 dumped loads of money into groupware and ERP offerings creating "strategic" advantages for themselves.

Well, NotWired is built for the other 99% of us. It is an integrated information system with the basic building blocks of groupware and wireless access. Members do not have to "purchase" and maintain expensive licenses of software for desktops to have all the advantages of groupware. Additionally, NotWired is committed to building group applications for your business with little to no cost.

NotWired gives small and medium business teams the strategic advantage so they can focus more efforts on business and not internal computer systems!
