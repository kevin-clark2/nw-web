Why do you have a company phonelist on paper?

Think about it for a minute. You want to look someone up in the company directory and you have a paper phone list? Why? And how up to date is it anyway with today’s employee turnover and change of locations and phone numbers?

Organizations spend time every month maintaining a phone list of work, home and mobile phone numbers, emails and addresses. Today’s mobile and distributed workforce changes this information by the minute. Why require someone in the organization to create and maintain a company phonebook, when each member can update their information over the Internet or wirelessly?

NotWired has organization phonebooks that allow members to make changes to their information on-line. Therefore, the phonebook can always be correct with little or no staff to maintain it. Now, with wireless access, any phone number in your organization phonebook is available to you instantly. Gone are the days where you call the receptionist to track down a number that is incorrect on the phonebook. If you need to call a colleague at home for that important question, you have the number at your fingertips.

NotWired (www.notwired.com), an on-line group and collaboration service for business professionals, is a unique offering to share and collaborate on common information like phonebooks. Additionally, it has group scheduling, group task management, shared bookmarks, workflow, status reports and more to run your organization. Best of all, its FREE!
