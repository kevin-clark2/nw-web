mission
NotWired's mission is to help small and midsize companies work efficiently so they can spend more time growing the business and less time running the business. We empower business with access anytime and anywhere.
   
overview NotWired is an application service provider that automates information systems for small and midsize companies. It is specifically designed for companies with highly mobile and distributed team members who need access to company information anytime. NotWired brings teams together with scheduling, task management and streamlining administrative tasks.
   
history and founding Based in Chicago, Illinois, NotWired was founded in July 2000. It was originally developed under Objectsoft's funding and is currently private.
   
market opportunity Small and medium businesses account for 8 million companies in the U.S. and 17 million worldwide. They constitute 98% of total companies, employ 75% of the workforce, and spend over 50% of all software expenditures. With 43% of the overall U.S. workforce mobile, wireless access to company information becomes critical. Mobile professionals will drive a large portion of the estimated $200 billion in wireless subscriptions, transactions and m-commerce by 2003.

NotWired is a solution for small and emerging businesses that does not require any installed software to manage front-office processing. Our market is estimated to be $300 billion in subscriptions, transactions and purchases.
