Kevin Clark, President and CEO, Board Member - Kevin has held sales positions at IBM, NeXT and ParcPlace. In all sales positions he achieved top awards for sales including the top ten new account sales reps in the country while at IBM. For the last few years, Kevin started and grown Objectsoft to a specialized consulting firm for fortune 1000 companies in telecommunications, transportation, insurance and healthcare. Broad industry experience and running a business was the genesis for the vision behind NotWired’s offering. From the age of 12, Kevin has programmed computers. He earned his B.S. in Computer Engineering at University of Illinois - Urbana.
