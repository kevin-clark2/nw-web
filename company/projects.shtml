Projects

NotWired believes in small project teams to enhance the focus and minimize communications complexity. Project teams consist of three to eight members that focus on solving problems quickly and professionally.

Projects include major e-business applications for clients, strategic technology developments for NotWired and tactical developments. All have characteristics that are best met by a certain type of team structure. This project approach is a key success factor at NotWired.
