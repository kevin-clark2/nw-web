Technology Development

NotWired is the brainchild of Objectsoft. It is an application service to assist mobile professionals with scheduling and coordinating groups of people including employees, customers and suppliers.

In 1996, Objectsoft started with visual components for Smalltalk. Before the advent of Java, Smalltalk was a rapidly growing language in the Fortune 1000. About a hundred Fortune 1000 clients purchased and use our development components for VisualWorks Smalltalk.

With the introduction of Java, Objectsoft pursued development of tools for the Java marketplace. The first versions of these tools are described in our products catalog.

The Internet moved in the direction of thin clients and devices. Devices attached to the Internet will eclipse the number of PCs in the marketplace today. For the next revolution Objectsoft provides the architecture design and implementation services to deliver custom, scaleable, strategic applications to the entire Internet user community.
