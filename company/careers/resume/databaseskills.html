Database Skill Definitions

  • none - I have no database experience whatsoever.
  • read a book - I have read a book on databases and SQL.
  • novice - I know SQL and have fired some queries on the database.
  • skilled - I have designed less than 5 database systems.
  • very skilled - I have designed more than 5 database systems.
  • guru - I have designed and administered lots of database systems.
