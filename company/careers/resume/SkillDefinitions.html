Skill Definitions

If you are not sure about the level of skills that you possess,
click the besides the skill to know exactly what each option means.
  • none - I have not used the technology at all.
  • read a book - I have read a book and written a small prototype.
  • novice - I have written some code for less than 3 months in the technology.
  • skilled - I have implemented a major commercial product in the technology.
  • very skilled - I have implemented a couple of major projects in the technology.
  • guru - Ask me anything about the API's, bugs in the API's, etc. I do not call
               for support because I may know more than the engineers who made it.
