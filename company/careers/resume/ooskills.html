OO Skill Definitions

  • none - What are design patterns?
  • read a book - I have read a book about it and written some example code.
  • novice - I have used some patterns but have to know them more.
  • skilled - I have implemented a major commercial product in the technology.
  • very skilled - I really know design patterns and apply them regularly.
  • guru - I know design patterns very well. I have used all of them once or the other.
