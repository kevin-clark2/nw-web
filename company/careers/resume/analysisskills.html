Analysis/Design Skill Definitions

  • none - I mainly write code other people specify
  • read a book - I have read about UML and know about use cases, sequence diagrams, collaborations, etc.
  • novice - I have used design tools for small design efforts.
  • skilled - I have implemented one major analysis and design using a methodology.
  • very skilled - I have analyzed and designed several major frameworks.
  • guru - I could write a book on this topic, I have read most major analysis and design theories and practice serveral.
