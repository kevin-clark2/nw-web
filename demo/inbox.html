NotWired.com is a complete mobile portal solution built for mobile professionals. Access to NotWired.com software is available through your mobile device and Internet browser.

To take the interactive tour, click on the hyperlinks on the mobile phone.

Inbox Your inbox contains incoming events, information and documents. From the inbox, you can accept, decline, forward or remove items. When taking actions on items in your inbox, you may choose from standard messages or custom messages created in preferences on  the NotWired.com site.
