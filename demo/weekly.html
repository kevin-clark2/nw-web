NotWired.com is a complete mobile portal solution built for mobile professionals. Access to NotWired.com software is available through your mobile device and Internet browser.

To take the interactive tour, click on the hyperlinks on the mobile phone.

Weekly A weekly calendar is listed Monday through Sunday. Users can page through weeks. Choose a day and a daily calendar view will display. Users can create an event, jump to a date or return to the main index from the options menu.

Times listed are determined by the preferences settings set on the NotWired.com site.
