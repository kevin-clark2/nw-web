NotWired.com is a complete mobile portal solution built for mobile professionals. Access to NotWired.com software is available through your mobile device and Internet browser.

To take the interactive tour, click on the hyperlinks on the mobile phone.

Todos Todos can be created from your mobile device or the NotWired.com site. You can create todos for yourself, or others in your organization. Todos may be marked complete, deferred with comments, erased, or reassigned to someone else in your organization. Users can find todos and jump to the main index from the options menu.

Comments are determined by the preferences settings set on the NotWired.com site.
