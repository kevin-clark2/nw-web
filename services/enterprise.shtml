Service Offering Details

NotWired works with enterprises to deliver world-class Internet and wireless applications. NotWired's experienced staff can assist in planning, designing and implementing proven technologies to deliver solutions on-time and within budget.

Some sample services include:

Strategy Consulting. NotWired will help your team analyze strategies to delivery solutions to your marketplace. Core compenticies include wireless strategy development, web application development, development and deployment processes. The scope of this engagement varies by client.
Project Planning and Management. NotWired's management team can help you and your team scope and manage the complexities of new technologies. NotWired can assist in the management of a development staff to complete a project on time and on budget. The scope of this engagement varies by client.
Portal Fast-Start. NotWired will drop in a complete development environment and all portal frameworks necessary to deliver enterprise applications within a month.
Architecture Services. NotWired will analyze your current architecture and technologies for readiness to deliver Internet and mobile applications to your internal and external users. The scope of this engagement varies by client.
Application Architecture Services. NotWired will create a common application architecture for various teams to develop new applications over the wired and wireless Internet.
Internet and Wireless Architecture Services. Developing scalable Internet solutions requires experience. NotWired's team will assist in creating an architecture for Internet and wireless Internet access in one unified architecture.
Data and Object Architecture Services. Often the heart of applications. The guts of a system need to be in place before development. NotWired's staff are object and data design experts.
Infrastructure Development. NotWired's staff can assist in the development of services that are infrastructure for other applications. Our experience on several projects makes this a low-risk effort.
Deployment Services. Deployment can be tricky and often delay a project. Operating a 24x7 operation requires significant investment. NotWired has the staff, facilities and pre-built infrastructure to rapidly deploy applications.
Outsourcing. NotWired's staff can lead the development of complex systems to deliver scalable Internet and wireless applications.
