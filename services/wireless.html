The NotWired solution helps wireless carriers retain and grow their most important asset, customers.

Problem Solution
How to grow revenues NotWired is an out of the box groupware solution for wireless. Businesses, families, friends and organizations can collaborate and communicate through Internet enabled phones.

NotWired is a driving reason for groups to use wireless Internet services. This will increase your per user revenues and introduce additional revenue channels from micro-transactions. A sample micro-transaction is a directory assistance call that is used on impulse for a transaction fee.

NotWired can define micro-transactions for any application on the NotWired system.

How to decrease churn When co-branding NotWired, the systems to support your user base are yours. Users cannot easily switch NotWired systems without manually transferring data with the exception of appointments and contacts.
No staff to develop services Internet services are difficult and expensive to develop. If you have an idea, the NotWired team has all the tools and skills to deliver value to your marketplace fast.

The standard NotWired system takes about one month to enter a Beta test with a new carrier. After that, you are ready to increase revenues and decrease churn!
