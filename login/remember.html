NotWired Login Help
What does "remember me" mean?

When you sign in using your NotWired login information, your browser can "save" this information. By checking the box you won't have to sign in each time you return to come back. We will, however, ask you to re-enter your password if you have been away from the computer too long.

Should I check it or not?

You can always sign out of NotWired.com at any time by clicking the "Logout" link that is present on every NotWired page. Once you have signed out, NotWired will ask for your organization, login and password again the next time you return.

Important: Do not check the box if you are accessing NotWired.com from a shared computer. DO NOT check the "Remember login" check box.

How does it work?

The login information is encrypted and stored as a cookie on your browser. More information is available about persistent cookies here.
